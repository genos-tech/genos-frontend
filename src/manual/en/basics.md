## What Genos is

Genos puts chat, tasks and notes in one workspace, with an AI called Genos that can read all three. You don't switch between a chat app, a task tracker and a wiki. Everything sits in one place and stays connected: a chat thread can become a task, a task has its own notes and discussion, and Genos can answer questions across all of it.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar has these entries, top to bottom:

- **Genos**: the Genos page, a full page for asking Genos questions or asking it to do work. Your earlier conversations are listed under Ask history, where you can search them and pin them. This entry is always shown.
- **Inbox**: things that are waiting for you (see below).
- **Chats**: direct messages, group chats and one chat for each project.
- **Tasks**: projects, tasks, milestones, sprints, the board and the table.
- **Notes**: My Notes, Team Notes, notes shared with you, and notes attached to tasks and chats.
- **To-Do**: your personal to-do list. It opens over the page you are on. Use **Move to page** to open the full list.
- **History**: the chats, tasks and notes you opened recently, on three tabs.
- **Settings** and **Sign out**.

Your team is at the top of the sidebar. Click it to switch teams if you belong to more than one. Your avatar at the bottom opens your profile.

You can hide Inbox, Chats, Tasks, Notes and To-Do in Settings → General → Sidebar. Genos is always shown. The choice syncs across your devices.

## The Inbox

The Inbox has two tabs:

- **Activities**: things that happened. These include mentions, replies, reactions and task updates. Reminders you set also land here when they come due, as do reports from your Genos Assist Schedules and the free-trial notice.
- **Requests**: things someone is waiting on you to decide. These include requests to join your team, project or group chat, and requests for access to your note. Team ownership claims, connection requests from other teams and items another team shared with yours land here too.

Use **Mark all as read** on Activities, and **Sync inbox** to fetch items that arrived while the page was open.

## Works everywhere

- Press Cmd+K on a Mac (Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux) anywhere to open Spotlight. Spotlight is the quick-ask box: type to search your chats, tasks and notes, or press Enter to ask Genos.
- On a phone, you can install Genos as an app. In Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. On an iPhone, notifications only work in the installed app.
- Choose the app language in Settings → General → Language: English, 日本語, Español, Français, 中文 or हिन्दी. Some translations may be incomplete. Anything missing shows in English.
- Choose Light, Dark or System under Settings → General → Appearance. System follows your device. You can also choose a color theme (the accent color) and a density (row height in lists and tables).
- Paste a Genos link to a task, milestone, note, chat or thread into a message. Clicking it opens a preview window over the page, so you don't lose your place. **Move to page** opens the item itself.
