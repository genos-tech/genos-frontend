## The three channels

- **In-app:** your Inbox, plus a toast when the tab is open but you are on another screen.
- **Push:** a desktop or phone notification when the tab is in the background or Genos is closed. Allow it next to "Browser permission" in Settings > Notifications. The device you are looking at does not get a push for news it already shows, but your other devices still do. On an iPhone, notifications work only after you add Genos to your Home Screen.
- **Email:** for when you are away. Genos waits until you have been away for a while, then sends one email with what you missed, not one email per event. Anything you read in the app before then is left out. Email is sent only when your Genos has email turned on.

## Settings > Notifications

- **Web notifications:** the main switch. When it is off, no category notifies you, and that includes email.
- **Pause notifications:** silences in-app, push and email notifications. Pause For 30 minutes, For 1 hour, For 2 hours, Until tomorrow, Until next week, or pick a date and time. Under On a schedule you can pause every day during set hours, for example 17:00 to 09:00. While you are paused, others see a moon on your avatar. The status menu on your avatar also has quick pause choices.
- **Groups:** Chats, Thread replies, Mentions, Task comments, Inbox, and What my AI apps do. Each group has its own switch, and some open to finer switches:
    - Mentions: one switch for each place you can be mentioned (chat message, thread reply, task description, task comment, My Notes, task note, chat note).
    - Inbox: requests and notices, AI answer ready (an AI answer finished after you closed its window), Message reminders and To-do reminders. If you turn a reminder switch off, the reminder still appears in your Inbox, just without a notification.
    - What my AI apps do: what AI apps connected to Genos (for example Claude) do for you. By default you are alerted only for risky actions: deletes, changes to who can open a note or who is in a project, many refused requests, a new app or API key connected, and changes to 10 or more items at once. You can turn on alerts for other actions too.
- **Email notifications:** a separate switch for email, with its own categories. These are on by default: Mentions in chat, Mentions in threads, Mentions in tasks, Mentions in notes, Thread replies, Task comments, and Requests & notices. Being assigned a task also emails you. "Every chat message" and "Reactions" are off by default. A finished AI answer is never emailed. If you turn off a group above, its emails stop too.
- **Muted chats** and **Muted items:** everything you have muted, with an Unmute button for each.

## Muting one conversation or item

- To mute a chat, click the bell in the chat header.
- To mute one thread, task or note, use its header or its ⋮ menu.

## Unsubscribing from email

Every notification email has an unsubscribe link. It turns email notifications off. You can turn them back on in Settings > Notifications.

The old daily email digest and the 8am Inbox digest no longer exist. For a regular summary, set up a Genos Assist Schedule in Settings > Genos (Core and above).
