## Fast moves

- **Cmd+K (Ctrl+K)** opens Spotlight from anywhere, to search or ask Genos.
- The letter shortcuts are Ctrl+Cmd+letter on a Mac and Ctrl+Alt+letter on Windows and Linux:
    - **T**: open Tasks and start a new task
    - **N**: create a new My Note
    - **X**: open the To-Do list
    - **H**: open History
    - **G**: open the task graph for the task in the preview
    - **C**: open the calendar
    - **M**: copy a new Google Meet link

    On Windows and Linux they do nothing while you type in a text field.

- **Hold Cmd and tap Ctrl** (Alt and Ctrl elsewhere) to cycle through Genos, Inbox, Chats, Tasks, Notes and To-Do.
- **Cmd+Z or Ctrl+Z** undoes a deletion while its Undo message is still showing.
- **/** on the Notes page jumps to the filter box of the notes list.
- **Cmd+Enter or Ctrl+Enter** sends a message or comment, and creates the task in the new-task form.
- The task table, the sprint board and the calendar work from the keyboard. Tab moves into them, and the arrow keys move inside.
- Settings → Shortcuts lists every shortcut. The list is read-only: shortcuts can't be changed.
- Paste a Genos link into a message, and clicking it opens a preview over the page, so you never lose your place.
- In a thread, **New task linked to this thread** turns a "we should do this" conversation into tracked work. A thread can have one task.
- The sprint board, the task table and the task graph are three views of the same tasks. Use the one that fits the moment.

## When something seems off

- **Not getting notifications?**
    - Open Settings → Notifications. Check that notifications are on, that they are not paused (a paused person has a moon on their avatar), and that the browser permission says Granted.
    - Check that the chat or item is not muted.
    - On an iPhone, install Genos to the home screen and allow notifications there.
- **Can't see a project, group chat or note?** You may not be a member. Ask the owner to add you, or use **Request to Join** or **Request Access** where it is offered.
- **Something looks out of date?** Reload the tab. A tab you signed in to another team from elsewhere also asks you to reload, because only one team can be active per browser.
