## Mac and Windows/Linux keys

Where a shortcut uses a modifier key, the Mac key and the Windows/Linux key often differ. Each shortcut below gives both. Settings > Shortcuts shows the same list for your computer, with any shortcuts you changed. The keys below are the built-in ones.

## Changing a shortcut

You can change eight shortcuts: Spotlight (Cmd-K / Ctrl-K) and the seven letter shortcuts for actions. In Settings > Shortcuts, select the keys of the shortcut, then press the new ones. The new keys must be Ctrl and Cmd (Ctrl and Alt on Windows/Linux) with one letter or number. Escape or Cancel stops without changing anything.

- Genos refuses keys that another shortcut already uses, and a few that the system takes (Ctrl-Cmd-Q, F and D on a Mac; Ctrl-Alt-D and L elsewhere). A browser or another app can still take some combinations before Genos sees them.
- A changed shortcut is marked Custom. Use its reset button, or Reset all, to get the built-in keys back.
- Changes are saved to your account. Mac keys and Windows/Linux keys are kept separately.
- On Windows and Linux, a changed shortcut does nothing while you type in an editor or text field, Spotlight included.

The other shortcuts (switching parts of Genos, undo, send, create task, chat arrows, the notes filter, and the table, board and calendar keys) cannot be changed.

## Anywhere in Genos

- Cmd-K (Ctrl-K): open Spotlight. On the Genos page it puts the cursor in that page's own box instead.
- Escape: closes whatever is on top: a menu, a window, or an @ list before the window it is in.
- Switch between parts of Genos: hold Cmd (Alt on Windows/Linux) and tap Ctrl. Each tap moves through Inbox, Chats, Tasks, Notes, Genos and To-Do in the order you last used them, so one tap takes you back to where you were. Add Shift to go backwards. Let go of the held key to switch, or press Escape to stay where you are.
- Cmd-Z (Ctrl-Z): undo a deletion while its Undo message is still showing.
- / on the Notes page: jump to the filter box of the notes list. It does nothing while you are typing in a text field.

## Jump straight to an action

Hold Ctrl and Cmd together on a Mac (Ctrl and Alt on Windows/Linux), then press a letter:

- T: open Tasks and start a new task.
- N: open Notes and start a new note in My Notes.
- X: open your To-Do list.
- C: open the compact calendar.
- M: copy a new Meet link to your clipboard.
- H: open History.
- G: open the task graph for the task you are previewing.

On Windows and Linux these letter shortcuts do nothing while you type in an editor or text field. That way the editor keeps its own Ctrl+Alt keys (Ctrl+Alt+C makes a code block).

## In the Spotlight / Genos box

- Up and Down: move through the results.
- Enter: open the highlighted result as a preview. If no result is highlighted, Enter sends your question to Genos.
- Shift-Enter: start a new line in a long question without sending it.
- Cmd-click (Ctrl-click) a result: open the full page instead of a preview.

## In chat

- Cmd-Enter or Ctrl-Enter: send. Either key works on every computer. Plain Enter starts a new line, so a half-written message is not sent by accident.
- The same keys send a thread reply or a task comment.
- Cmd-click (Alt-click on Windows/Linux) a message: open its thread without leaving the conversation.
- Cmd-Shift-Left/Right (Alt-Shift-Left/Right on Windows/Linux): move between chat tabs. Use Up/Down instead to move through the chat list. These do nothing while you are typing in a field.

## Creating tasks

- Quick-add row: Enter creates the task, and Escape removes the row.
- The full new-task form: Cmd-Enter on a Mac, Ctrl-Enter on Windows/Linux. Unlike chat, only that one key works on each computer.

## Task table

Press Tab to move into the table or out of it. Inside the table:

- Arrow keys: move between cells.
- Home / End: go to the first or last cell in the row. Ctrl+Home / End: go to the first or last cell in the table.
- PageUp / PageDown: move a page up or down.
- Enter or Space: open the cell's editor. Esc: close the editor without saving.

## Task board

Tab moves between columns, one stop per column. Inside the board:

- Arrow keys: move between cards and columns.
- Home / End: go to the first or last card in the column. Ctrl+Home / End: go to the first or last card on the board.
- Enter: open the card's preview.
- Space: pick up a card. Then the arrow keys move it, Space drops it, and Esc cancels.

## Calendar

Tab moves into the calendar and out of it. The month view is one stop. The week and day views have one stop per day.

- Arrow keys: move between days and weeks (month view), or between events and days (week and day views).
- Home / End: first or last day of the week (month view), or first or last event of the day (week and day views).
- PageUp / PageDown: previous or next month (month view).
- Enter: open the day's events or the event. On an empty day, Enter starts a new event.
