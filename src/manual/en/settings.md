## Opening Settings

Click Settings in the left sidebar. On a phone, tap More, then Settings. Settings opens on top of what you were doing, so you do not lose your place. It has 14 tabs, in this order: General, Plan & Usage, AI credit usage, Genos, Chat, Tasks, Notifications, Mention groups, Custom emoji, Shortcuts, Security, Account, Integrations, Developer. The "Settings A-Z" page of this manual lists every setting with who can change it, the plan it needs and its default.

## General

- **Appearance:** Theme (Light, Dark or System; System follows your device and changes with it), Color theme (the accent color), and Density (row height in lists and tables).
- **Language:** English, 日本語, Español, Français, 中文 or हिन्दी. Genos also answers you in this language. Text that is not translated yet appears in English.
- **Sidebar:** show or hide Inbox, Chats, Tasks, Notes and To-Do in the left sidebar. Genos is always shown. This syncs across your devices.
- **Privacy & analytics:** Share usage data. It is off until you accept.

## Plan & Usage

Your plan, how much of each limit you have left, and buttons to upgrade, switch or cancel your plan or open Manage billing. Only the team owner sees the Team plan section (per-seat billing for the whole team). Where offered, you can buy one-off credit packs here. Bought credits never expire.

## AI credit usage

Your own AI credits: how many are left, what you used this month, which jobs used them, and recent charges. If your plan counts daily AI requests instead of credits, this tab says so.

## Genos

Settings for the AI (the tab was called Spotlight before):

- AI model (or Effort)
- Genos answers (AI answers and Web search)
- How much Genos writes
- Genos Assist Schedules
- What Genos remembers
- Skills

The Skills card appears only when your Genos has Skills turned on. See the Genos AI page of this manual for what each one does.

## Chat

- **Message layout:** Bubble or Compact.
- **Quick reactions:** your three one-click emoji.
- **Message actions:** pin up to 4 actions to the hover toolbar of messages. The rest stay in the … menu.
- **Double-click action:** whether double-clicking a message adds it to your To-Do list.

## Tasks

- **Quick task creation:** whether the quick-add row in the task table requires the project's required fields.
- **Auto-close on PR merge:** close your tasks when their pull request merges. This needs GitHub connected.
- **Auto-sync to Google Calendar:** add your tasks that have due dates to Google Calendar as all-day events, and Sync existing tasks now. This needs Google Calendar connected.

## Notifications

The main switch, Pause notifications, browser permission, notification groups, Email notifications, and your muted chats and items. See the Notifications page of this manual.

## Mention groups and Custom emoji

- **Mention groups:** a name such as @design that notifies every member of the group when it is mentioned in chat, task comments or task descriptions.
- **Custom emoji:** your team's own emoji. Names use a-z, 0-9, \_, + and -, up to 50 characters. Files must be .png, .jpg, .gif, .webp or .svg, up to 512 KB. Anyone on the team can add one. Only the person who uploaded an emoji can delete it.

## Shortcuts

A list of the keyboard shortcuts. You can change Spotlight and the seven letter shortcuts: select a shortcut's keys, press Ctrl and Cmd (Ctrl and Alt on Windows/Linux) with a letter or number, and reset it any time. The rest are fixed. See the Keyboard shortcuts page of this manual.

## Security

Two-step verification. When it is on, Genos asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app after your password. You get recovery codes for when you lose your phone. It applies to email-and-password sign-in. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, turn on two-step verification in that account instead. See the Security and sign-in page of this manual.

## Account

- **Export your data:** download what you created as a JSON file.
- **Your security activity:** your recent sign-ins, sign-outs, data exports, connected accounts and API keys, plus failed attempts to sign in to your account.
- **Your teams:** leave a team. If you own the team, transfer ownership from its profile first.
- **Delete your account:** type DELETE (and your password, if your account has one). Genos will not delete your account while you own a team that has other members.

## Integrations

Connect Google (for Google Calendar) and GitHub, and choose which repositories Genos can see. The tab is there only when integrations are turned on in your Genos.

## Developer

Connect your AI (Claude or ChatGPT, from Core up), Connected apps, and personal API keys. Creating a key asks for your password again. Team owners and editors also see team keys, connected AI apps and webhooks. Owners also see AI app use and the Security log. Webhooks are not included on Free. See the Connecting page of this manual.

## Your profile (not part of Settings)

Click your avatar and choose My Profile to change your picture, name, job title and status. The status menu on your avatar also lets you pause notifications or choose Set Always Offline.

## Things people look for that aren't there

- Changing your password while signed in: there is no screen for this. Use the forgotten-password link on the sign-in page.
- Changing your email address: not available in the app.
- A time zone picker: Genos reads your time zone from your device. You can see it under Settings > Genos > What Genos remembers > Settings Genos uses.
- Task table columns and sorting: these are in the task table's own column settings, not in Settings.
