## Kinds of conversations

The chat list has these sections: Direct Messages, Group Messages, Project Updates, Flagged Messages and Recent Activities.

- **Direct messages (DM)**: one-to-one. For a quick talk with several people, start a multi-user DM (at least 3 people) without creating a group.
- **Group messages**: named group chats. When you create one, you choose whether it is public or private. A private group can also be hidden, so only its members can see that it exists. **Browse group chats** lists the public groups you can join. To join a private group, you send a request, and the group's members get it in their Inbox.
- **Project chats** (under Project Updates): every project has one. Task updates appear there as cards. Click a card to open the task.

The owner of a group can archive it. An archived group keeps everything but is read-only until it is unarchived. The owner can also delete the group.

## Everyday messaging

- **Threads:** use **Reply in thread** to keep side discussions out of the main flow. A thread opens in its own pane. Cmd+click a message on a Mac (Alt+click elsewhere) to open its thread.
- **Mentions:** type @ to mention someone. They are notified in the app and by push. If your Genos has email notifications turned on, they also get an email while they are away. A mention group lets one @ reach a set of people. Any team member except guests manages them in Settings → Mention groups.
- **Reactions:** hover a message to pick one of the three quick reactions, or open the emoji picker. Change the quick three in Settings → Chat → Quick reactions.
- **Custom emoji and GIFs:** any team member can upload custom emoji in Settings → Custom emoji (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or SVG, up to 512 KB). Only the person who uploaded an emoji can delete it. Type /gif in the message editor to search GIPHY, when your Genos has it turned on.
- **Flags:** use **Flag for later** to keep a message as a personal follow-up. Flagged messages are listed under Flagged Messages. **Mark done** when you have handled one, and use **View completed flags** to see past ones.
- **Reminders:** use **Remind me…** on a message to pick a time, such as in 1 hour or tomorrow morning. When it comes due, you get a notification and an item in your Inbox. Task comments have the same option.
- **To-Do:** **Add to To-Do** puts a message on your personal to-do list. Double-clicking a message does the same, unless you turn it off in Settings → Chat → Double-click action.
- **Ask Genos about this message** asks Genos with that message attached. You can also select text inside a message and ask about just that text.
- **Copy message link** gives a link that opens that exact message.

## Your chat list and layout

- Pin a chat to keep it at the top of your list. **Remove from sidebar** hides a chat for you only, until a new message arrives.
- Group chats can carry personal tags. Tags are private to you, and you can filter the group list by them. The **Unread** filter shows only chats with unread messages.
- Recent Activities shows the last 30 days of replies, mentions, reactions and task activity, with filters.
- Mute a chat with the bell icon in its header.
- **Message layout** in Settings → Chat chooses Compact (full-width rows, the default) or Bubble.
- **Message actions** in Settings → Chat chooses up to 4 actions to keep on the toolbar that appears when you hover a message. The rest stay in the … menu.

## Finding things again

- Press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K) to search messages, tasks and notes, or ask Genos in plain language.
- Search people and groups from the box at the top of the chat list.
- On some plans, older messages are hidden. A banner in the chat says so.
