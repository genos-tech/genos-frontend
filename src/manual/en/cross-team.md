## What this is for

Sometimes the people you need in a chat, a project or a folder of notes work for another company: a client, an agency, a contractor. Genos lets you share one item with another team. They do not get into the rest of your workspace, and nobody needs a second account.

It works in two steps. First you connect the two teams, once. Then you share single items. A connection on its own gives the other team access to nothing.

## Step 1: connect the two teams

1. Ask the other company for their Team ID. They find it under Team ID in their Team Profile (team menu > **Show Team Profile**). On its own the Team ID grants nothing.
2. Open your own Team Profile, go to **Connected teams**, paste their ID and choose **Request connection**. The row shows "Waiting for them to approve".
3. Their team owner gets a **Team connection** card in their Inbox, under **Requests**. An owner or editor of their team can also answer from **Connected teams** in their own Team Profile, with **Approve** or **Decline**.
4. Once approved, each team sees the other under Connected teams as "Connected". A chip on the row says who asked: **Owner** means they invited your team, **Guest** means your team invited them. Who owns a shared item is shown on the item itself.

- You do this once per company, however much you share later.
- Only an owner or editor can request or approve a connection. Anyone in the team can see the list.
- Nothing arrived? Check that they sent their Team ID (not a user ID or a project ID). The card goes to their team owner.

## Step 2: share one item

Only an owner or editor of your team can share an item.

- **A chat**: when you create a group chat, turn on **Include another organization** and pick connected teams. A group chat that already exists cannot be shared: create a new one that includes the other team. A one-to-one DM, a multi-person DM and a project chat cannot be shared. An external chat is always private.
- **A project**: open the project profile and use **Teams with access** > **Share with a connected team**. Its tasks, task comments, files, task notes, milestones and project chat come with it.
- **A note folder**: in a Team Notes folder's access settings, choose **Share with another organization** (offered only when your team has a connection) and pick connected teams. A shared folder is always restricted to the people let in.
- When you share, you choose what they can do: **Edit** or **View only**; **View only** is pre-selected, so give Edit only when they need to change things. That is the highest role their people can get on that item, and changing it on a share that is already running takes effect at once.
- With **View only** their people can open and read the item but not change it. On a project they cannot create or edit tasks, comment, upload files, edit task notes or post in the project chat. In a chat they cannot post, edit, delete or react. In a note folder they read the notes. With **Edit** they can do these things.
- On a project, people from the other team always join as guests, whatever you chose: they cannot manage its members.
- The other team's owner gets a **Shared with your team** card in their Inbox. An owner or editor of that team accepts it. The person who accepts is let in at once, so they can open the item and decide who else on their side needs it.

## Where the shared item turns up

You find it in your own workspace, in the usual place for that kind of item.

- A shared project appears in your project list, marked as shared. Hover the mark to see which team shared it.
- An external chat appears in your chat list with an **External** badge.
- A shared note folder appears in your Team Notes with a **Shared** badge.
- Only people who were let in see it. Colleagues who were not added see nothing.
- Nothing there? Someone on your team has to add you. Ask whoever accepted the share.

## Each side manages its own people

After a share is accepted, the other team's owner or editor adds and removes their own colleagues on that item whenever they like. They do not need to ask you again.

- Open the item (the project profile, the chat profile or the folder's access settings) and find **Teams with access**. Your own team's row has **Add someone from your team**.
- They can add only people who are members of their own team, and never above the role you allowed.
- You can remove any single person from another team at any time. You cannot add their people for them.
- When someone leaves the other team, they lose access to your shared items at once.

## What stays with the owning team

- Renaming or deleting the shared project, chat or folder, and changing who in the owning team can reach it.
- Sharing it further. A team you shared with cannot pass the item to a third team, and can add only its own members.
- Ending the share. Either team can choose **Stop sharing with this team**. Everyone that team had on the item loses access at once. What they wrote inside stays with the owning team.

## What people from the other team do not see

- Only what was shared: that project, not your other projects; that chat, not your other chats; that folder, not your other notes. Never your member list.
- Search and Genos follow the same boundary. They get results and answers only from the items they were let into.
- Each person's AI use counts against their own plan and credits. Sharing an item does not give anyone your plan.

## Ending a connection

- Stop sharing one item from its **Teams with access** section.
- To end everything, a team owner (on either side) chooses **Disconnect** under Connected teams. This ends every chat, project and note folder shared between the two teams, in both directions. Genos asks you to confirm, then says how many people were removed.
- Either side can end a share or a connection without the other's agreement. You can connect again later, but the old shares do not come back by themselves.
