## Find your team's settings

Open the team menu and choose **Show Team Profile**. The Team Profile shows the team name, the Team ID, the owner, the members, pending invites, **Connected teams**, and, for the owner, **Sample data** and **Import from Slack**.

## Add people

- **Invite by email.** In the Team Profile, choose **Invite members**, type one or more addresses and choose **Send invites**. Each person gets a link that works only for their own email address. The link expires after 7 days. New members join as Viewers. Only the team owner or an editor can invite.
- **Pending invites** lists the invites that are not yet accepted, who sent each one and when it expires. An owner or editor can cancel one, and the link stops working at once.
- **Request to join.** Someone outside the team can open **Team Management**, search by Team ID and choose **Request to Join**. The request goes to the team owner's Inbox, under **Requests**. An owner or editor can approve it.
- **Guests.** To bring in someone from outside the company for particular projects only, open a project and choose **Invite guests**. A guest sees only the projects they were invited to. Guests do not see your other projects, your member list or anything else in the team. They do not get the team's plan. A project owner or editor can invite guests.
- Each person's time zone comes from their browser. Each person picks their own language under Settings > **General** > Language.

## Roles

Teams, projects and group chats use the same four roles.

- **Owner**: one per team, project or group chat. Only the owner can transfer ownership. In a team, only the owner can remove an editor, disconnect a connected team, remove sample data, import from Slack, buy a team plan and see the team's **Security log**.
- **Editor**: helps run the team day to day. Editors invite and add members, cancel invites, rename the team, change its image, set members' roles (Editor or Viewer), remove viewers and guests, and request or approve team connections. An editor cannot remove another editor or the owner. Make a second person an Editor early, so the team does not depend on one account.
- **Viewer**: the default for new members. Viewers work in the team's chats, projects and notes they belong to. They cannot invite, remove or change members, and they cannot edit the team.
- **Guest**: an outside collaborator invited to particular projects. Guests can work in the projects they were invited to and see nothing else.

In a project, the owner and editors can change **Customize Task Fields** (required fields and default values for new tasks and milestones).

## Remove people, leave a team

- To remove a member, an owner or editor opens the Team Profile and chooses **Remove** next to the person. Genos asks whether to unassign their open work. The owner cannot be removed: transfer ownership first.
- To leave a team yourself, open Settings > **Account** > **Your teams** and choose **Leave**. You lose access to that team's chats, projects and notes. Your account and your other teams stay. The owner cannot leave: transfer ownership first.
- There is no screen for deleting a team.

## Hand over or recover ownership

- **Transfer ownership**: the owner opens the Team Profile, chooses **Transfer ownership** and picks a member. The old owner becomes a regular member.
- **Request ownership**: if the owner has left or stopped responding, an editor can choose **Request ownership**. The owner gets an **Ownership claim** card in their Inbox and can approve or decline it.
- If the owner does neither within 30 days, the editor can choose **Take ownership**. Everyone in the team is told when that happens.
- If the owner declines, the same editor can ask again only after 60 days.
- A team with only an owner and viewers has nobody who can request ownership. Give someone the Editor role before you need it.

## Other team tools

- **Connected teams**: connect with another company's team so you can share single chats, projects or note folders with them. See "Working with another company: cross-team sharing".
- **Import from Slack** (owner only): upload a Slack workspace export ZIP to bring its channels, messages and threads into the team as group chats. People are matched to members by email address. Direct messages are not imported. Files become links back to Slack. **Undo this import…** removes what an import added.
- **Sample data** (owner only): a team created with "Include sample instruction data" gets example projects, chats, notes and to-dos. **Remove sample data…** shows what will be removed and what will stay, then removes it in one step. Anything you added or changed stays.

## Billing for a team

Billing is under Settings > **Plan & Usage**. Each person has their own plan. The team owner can also buy a per-seat team plan (Team Core, Team Pro or Team Max) on the plans page; every member then gets at least that plan. See "Plans, limits, and what happens when you hit one".
