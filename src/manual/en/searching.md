## One box, two jobs

Press Cmd-K (Ctrl-K) to open Spotlight. As you type, Genos searches your messages, tasks, notes and to-dos and shows the results below the box. No AI answer is written while you type. When you press Enter or click Ask, Genos reads the matching material and answers your question. Use a few words to search, and a full sentence to ask.

If AI answers are turned off in Settings > Genos, the box only searches.

## Narrowing a search

- Use the chips under the box to show only Chats, Tasks, Notes, Todos or Genos answers (answers Genos gave before).
- Use the project picker to limit results to one or more projects. Todos and Genos answers have no project, so you cannot use them together with a project filter.
- There is no search syntax. Quotation marks, AND, OR, a leading minus and field:value are read as ordinary words. Use the chips instead. Search matches meaning as well as exact words, so a plain phrase usually works best.
- To find one task, type only its id, for example MIP-890. That task appears at the top.
- Archived items still show up, marked Archived. Items in a deleted project do not show up.
- You only ever see what your account can open, and so does Genos.

## Asking well

- Name things in the question: @ for a person, # for a task, project, note or chat. "What's left on #WRD before Friday?" works better than "what's left".
- Ask where the material is. A thread and a note each have their own Ask button. A task has "Ask Genos about this task" in its ... menu. These limit the question to that one item.
- Answers link to their sources. Click a link to check it.
- Click a search result to open a preview over your list, so you keep your place. Cmd-click (Ctrl-click) opens the full page instead.
- Save a good answer with Save as note. It goes to My Notes and can be found by search later.

## When it can't find something

- New content takes a moment to become searchable. If something you just made does not show up, wait a little and try again.
- Web search is separate from workspace search. Turn it on under Web search in Settings > Genos. It is included from Core up. When it is off, Genos answers only from your workspace.

## Finding an answer you already had

The Genos page lists your past conversations under Ask history. It shows your 20 most recent conversations. Two things reach past that:

- **Search past asks.** The search box above the list matches every question in a conversation, not just the first one shown on the row. It searches your whole history, not only the 20 rows on screen.
- **Pin.** Click Pin this ask on a row to keep it at the top under Pinned, above Recent. Pinned conversations do not count toward the 20 recent ones. Click Unpin to remove the pin.

How far back your history goes depends on your plan: 30 days on Free, 180 days on Core, 365 days on Pro, and no limit on Max. Pinning does not keep a conversation past that limit. The conversation is hidden, not deleted, and it comes back (still pinned) if you upgrade.
